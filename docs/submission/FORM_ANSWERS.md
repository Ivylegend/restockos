## What did you build?

Small retailers lose sales when stock runs out, while manual purchasing takes time and makes spending hard to control. RestockOS monitors inventory, requests three supplier quotations, compares price, delivery and reliability, negotiates a compliant offer and creates a purchase order. A NEAR Testnet policy contract enforces approved suppliers, per-order caps, total budgets, pause controls and replay protection, with final transactions and receipts visible in the dashboard. The public sandbox runs the procurement workflow with clearly labeled simulated orders. Owner wallet authorization and the preserved restricted admin signer support live Testnet execution. Supplier integrations are deterministic demo adapters; the agent supports optional LLM recommendations with a deterministic fallback.
