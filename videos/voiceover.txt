RestockOS helps independent retailers prevent stockouts with controlled, autonomous procurement.

This Northline Electronics workspace tracks sixteen products. The dashboard highlights three items below their reorder points, while the inventory view shows real stock and daily sales estimates.

The USB-C charger is down to three units against a reorder point of twenty. The agent checks coverage, requests three supplier quotes, compares price, delivery and reliability, then negotiates inside the business policy.

The supplier table shows each offer and the selected compliant account. Spending limits and the supplier allowlist are enforced by the policy contract, not by the language model.

Visitors can run the public sandbox to follow the workflow. Those orders are clearly marked simulated and never move NEAR.

For a live Testnet purchase, the workspace owner connects a NEAR wallet, funds their own treasury and confirms the order in the wallet. RestockOS verifies the final transaction and contract receipt before showing a success status.

The recorded rejection shows what happens when a supplier tries to redirect payment: the contract rejects it and treasury funds remain protected.

RestockOS: a practical procurement agent, with clear workspace ownership and financial policy at every step.
